What can I book at Surf & Retreat Hostel Weligama?

Rooms: dorm beds and private rooms, from $12/night to from $55/night. Surf camp: lessons and coaching from $149 / week, plus board rental. Coworking & coliving: desk passes from $8 per day, and a bed-plus-desk coliving bundle for longer stays. All three are a five-minute walk from Weligama Bay, and you can combine them in one booking.
